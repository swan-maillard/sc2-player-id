    About the data

    StarCraft II is a real-time strategy game. Good players use hotkeys a lot: they bind groups of units or buildings to the keys 0 to 9 and switch between them dozens of times per minute. We had 3,052 games from 200 players, each one written as a single line of tokens, from a few tokens to more than 10,000.

    A model needs the same columns for every game, so we cut each game at 25 minutes and split it in three phases: start (first minute), early (up to 6 minutes) and mid (up to 25). In each phase we count, per second, the presses of each hotkey slot, the hotkeys created and used, and the selections. That's 15 rates for 3 phases, plus 3 columns for the race.

    The real games were on Kaggle, behind the class competition, so they aren't here. The logs you can load above are the only parts our notebooks printed: the first seconds of ten games, and the example from the competition page. It's already enough to see a habit: training games 0, 2 and 4 are all player 53, and all three create hotkey 3 in the first seconds.
